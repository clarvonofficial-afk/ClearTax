How PAYE / APIT works in Sri Lanka

Advance Personal Income Tax (APIT) is the current system used by Sri Lanka's Inland Revenue Department (IRD) to collect income tax on employment income, deducted monthly by your employer. APIT replaced the older PAYE (Pay As You Earn) framework, though the two terms are still often used interchangeably.

For the Year of Assessment 2025/2026 (effective 1 April 2025), the first Rs 1,800,000 of annual income is tax-free. Income above that is taxed progressively — meaning each portion of your salary is taxed at the rate for its own band, not your whole salary at one flat rate.

EPF and ETF contributions

Alongside income tax, employees contribute 8% of salary to the Employees' Provident Fund (EPF), matched by a 12% employer contribution. Employers additionally contribute 3% to the Employees' Trust Fund (ETF), which has no employee-side contribution.
